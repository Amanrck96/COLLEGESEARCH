## 1. Executive Summary & Defect Resolutions

Batch 39 marks the expansion of the verification pipeline into Bihar, alongside concluding critical Andhra Pradesh institutions, advancing the verified total to **390 Colleges (39.0% of 1,000 Roadmap)**. Crucial corrections included restoring Public/Government status for 4 major Bihar public and constituent institutions that had been misclassified as Private, purging cross-state photo leaks, fixing name typos, and eliminating raw Google search URLs.

### Key Corrections:
1. **Public & Constituent Institutions Restored (Bihar)**:
   - **Development Management Institute (DMI Patna, ID 3087)**: Restored Public/Government (Autonomous) classification supported by the Government of Bihar and approved by AICTE. Updated website to `https://www.dmi.ac.in`.
   - **University Department of Computer Applications, TMBU Bhagalpur (ID 3094)**: Restored Public/Government State University Department classification under Tilka Manjhi Bhagalpur University. Updated portal to `https://udcatmbu.org`.
   - **Bindeshwar Singh (B.S.) College, Danapur (ID 3116)**: Restored Public/Government Constituent College status (Estd 1954) under Patliputra University. Purged raw Google search query from website field and restored official portal `https://bscollegedanapur.ac.in`.
   - **Department of Business Administration, Sachchidanand Sinha College, Aurangabad (ID 3117)**: Restored Public/Government Constituent College status (Estd 1944, NAAC B++) under Magadh University. Updated portal to `https://ssinhacollege.co.in`.
   - **College of Commerce, Arts & Science, Patna (ID 3129)**: Fixed crawler name typo (`SCEINCE` -> `Science`). Restored Public/Government Constituent College status (Estd 1949, NAAC 'A', CPE) under Patliputra University. Updated website to `https://cocaspatna.ac.in`.
2. **Purged Cross-State Photo Leaks**:
   - **ID 3076 (Akshay IM Kadapa)**: Sailee College Borivali Mumbai (Maharashtra) leak purged.
   - **ID 3099 (ISM Patna)**: PES Bangalore (Karnataka) leak purged.
   - **ID 3104 (IBM Bela Darbhanga)**: VJTI Mumbai (Maharashtra) leak purged.
3. **Scraper Hotlink Replacement**:
   - Replaced 4 external scraper webp/hotlinks (collegedekho, kollegeapply) with clean local campus architecture assets.
