## 1. Executive Summary & Defect Resolutions

Batch 35 targeted 10 technical, management, and degree institutions across Andhra Pradesh, achieving the **350 Verified Colleges (35.0%) Milestone**. 4. **Course Duration Hallucination Resolved**:
