## 1. Executive Summary & Defect Resolutions

Batch 37 targeted 10 technical, engineering, and management institutions across Andhra Pradesh, advancing the verified total to **370 Colleges (37.0% of 1,000 Roadmap)**. A major finding in this batch was a severe cross-institution domain conflict (AMU domain assigned to PSCMRCET Vijayawada) alongside cross-state photo leaks.

### Key Corrections:
1. **Severe Domain Conflict & Cross-State Photo Leak Purged**:
   - **ID 2789 (PSCMRCET Vijayawada)**: Purged Aligarh Muslim University (UP) website (`https://www.amu.ac.in`) erroneously assigned to this Vijayawada college and restored official portal `https://pscmr.ac.in`. Purged COEP Pune Maharashtra photo leak. Verified NAAC 'A' grade and JNTUK affiliation.
2. **Purged Cross-State Photo Leaks**:
   - **ID 2789 (PSCMRCET Vijayawada)**: COEP Pune (Maharashtra) leak purged.
   - **ID 2791 (Tripura Vidya Mandiram Tadepalligudem)**: Chetana Bandra Mumbai (Maharashtra) leak purged.
3. **Autonomous & High-Accreditation Institutions Verified**:
   - **ID 2785 (Santhiram Engineering College Nandyal)**: Verified Autonomous status, NAAC Grade 'A+', NBA accreditation, affiliated to JNTUA.
   - **ID 2784 (ISTS for Women Rajanagaram)**: Verified NAAC Grade 'A+' accreditation, affiliated to JNTUK.
   - **ID 2780 (ALITS Anantapur)**: Verified NAAC Grade 'A' accreditation, affiliated to JNTUA.
4. **Scraper Hotlink Replacement**:
   - Replaced 6 external scraper webp/hotlinks (collegedunia, vidyavision, collegebatch) with clean local campus architecture assets.
