## 1. Executive Summary & Defect Resolutions

Batch 10 achieves the **100-College Official Verification Milestone**, auditing 10 key technical, management, and comprehensive higher education institutions across Uttar Pradesh (Agra, Kanpur, Gorakhpur, Ghaziabad, Greater Noida, and Lucknow).

### Key Forensic Findings & High-Impact Corrections:
1. **Critical Public / State University Misclassifications Restored**:
   - **Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar University (DBRAU), Khandari Campus, Agra (ID 784)**: Originally misclassified as `Private`. Established in 1927 as Agra University, DBRAU is one of Uttar Pradesh's historic **Public / State Universities**. Restored to `Public/Government (State University)` with `Government` ownership.
   - **Chhatrapati Shahu Ji Maharaj University (CSJMU), Kanpur (ID 785)**: Originally misclassified as `Private`. Established in 1966, CSJMU is a premier **State University** of Uttar Pradesh holding UGC Category-I status and NAAC A++ accreditation. Restored to `Public/Government (State University)` with `Government` ownership and subsidized fee structure (~₹45,000/yr).
2. **Major Cross-State Image Leaks Eliminated**:
   - **DBRAU Agra (ID 784)**: Was displaying `/images/campuses/bms_bangalore.jpg` (BMS College of Engineering in Bangalore, Karnataka being shown for a historic state university in Agra, UP!).
   - **CSJMU Kanpur (ID 785)**: Was displaying `/images/campuses/sheila_raheja_bandra.webp` (Sheila Raheja School of Architecture in Bandra, Mumbai being shown for CSJM University in Kanpur, UP!).
   - **Adhunik Institute Ghaziabad (ID 808)**: Was displaying `/images/campuses/iiit_bangalore_campus.jpg` (IIIT Bangalore campus photo being displayed for an institute in Duhai, Ghaziabad!).
   All three remapped to clean unbranded representative architectural assets (`campus_architecture_*.jpg`) with the automated "Representative Image" badge and tooltip.
3. **Broken URLs & Fragile Aggregator Hotlinks Purged**:
   - **Adhunik Institute Ghaziabad (ID 808)**: Had a raw Google Search URL (`https://www.google.com/search?q=ADHUNIK...`) as its official website! Replaced with verified official domain `https://adhunikgroup.org`.
   - Purged fragile aggregator hotlinks (Collegebatch webp, Getmyuni, Careerindia) on IPEM Ghaziabad (ID 777), MLRSM Lucknow (ID 782), IIF Business School Greater Noida (ID 797), and SRM Business School Lucknow (ID 814).
