### Key Forensic Findings & High-Impact Corrections:
1. **Critical Central Government Autonomous Institution Restored**:

2. **Four Severe Cross-State Image Leaks Eliminated**:
   - **Taxila Business School, Mansarovar, Jaipur (ID 1039)**: Was displaying `/images/campuses/gnims_matunga.png` (GNIMS Matunga Mumbai being shown for Taxila Business School in Jaipur!).
   - **Shankara International School of Management & Research, Kukas, Jaipur (ID 1051)**: Was displaying `/images/campuses/bangalore_university.jpg` (Bangalore University photo being shown in Kukas, Jaipur!).
   - **Champaran College of Professional Education, East Champaran, Bihar (ID 1093)**: Was displaying `/images/campuses/campus_cu_shah_mumbai.png` (CU Shah College Mumbai being shown in Champaran, Bihar!).
   - **Rungta Institute of Technology & Management, Forbesganj, Araria, Bihar (ID 1100)**: Was displaying `/images/campuses/campus_cu_shah_mumbai.png` (CU Shah College Mumbai being shown in Forbesganj, Bihar!).
   All four cross-institution leaks have been replaced with unbranded representative architectural assets (`campus_architecture_*.jpg`) carrying the automated "Representative Image" badge with tooltip.
3. **Raw Google Search URL & Fragile Hotlinks Purged**:
   - **Vinayak Institute of Management, Karnal (ID 906)**: Replaced a raw Google Search URL (`https://www.google.com/search?q=VINAYAK...`) with official institutional portal `https://www.vipmknl.com`.
