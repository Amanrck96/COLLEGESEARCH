## 1. Executive Summary & Defect Resolutions

Batch 34 targeted 10 technical and management institutions across Andhra Pradesh, resolving multiple cross-state photo leaks, historic aided institution status misclassifications, fake crawler domain placeholders, and degree duration errors.

### Key Corrections:
1. **Historic Autonomous Aided Institutions Restored**:
   - **School of Management Studies - M.R.P.G. College, Vizianagaram (ID 2661)**: Restored Autonomous Government-Aided status under the legendary Maharajah Alak Narayana Society of Arts and Science (MANSAS) Trust (Estd 1857/1987). Purged SVKM Usha Pravin Gandhi Vile Parle Mumbai photo leak.
   - **The Hindu College (PG Courses) Machilipatnam (ID 2663)**: Restored historic Government-Aided status (Estd 1928, NAAC A) affiliated with Krishna University. Purged raw Google Search query URL stored as website (`https://www.google.com/search?q=...`) and restored official portal `http://www.hcmtm.ac.in`. Purged VNIT Nagpur Maharashtra photo leak.
2. **Purged Cross-State Photo Leaks**:
   - **ID 2661 (MRPG Vizianagaram)**: SVKM Usha Pravin Gandhi College of Management Vile Parle (Mumbai, Maharashtra) leak purged.
   - **ID 2663 (The Hindu College Machilipatnam)**: VNIT Nagpur (Maharashtra) leak purged.
   - **ID 2667 (K. Chandrakala PG College Tenali)**: Bangalore University (Karnataka) leak purged.
3. **Course Duration Defects Resolved**:
   - **ID 2654 (PVKK Institute of Technology)**: Fixed BCA duration hallucination (4 Years -> 3 Years).
4. **Name Spelling & Typo Fixes**:
   - **ID 2659**: Corrected corrupted crawler string `VEERAVALLI VIDY SUNDAR P.G. COLLEGE` to `Veeravalli Vidya Sundar P.G. College`.
5. **Asset Modernization**:
   - Replaced 5 external scraper webp/hotlinks (getmyuni, universitykart, collegedekho, vidyavision, collegebatch) with clean local campus architecture assets.
