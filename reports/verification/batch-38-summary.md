## 1. Executive Summary & Defect Resolutions

Batch 38 targeted 10 institutions across Andhra Pradesh, advancing the verified total to **380 Colleges (38.0% of 1,000 Roadmap)**. A major finding in this batch was a prestigious National Deemed University (Indian Institute of Foreign Trade - IIFT Kakinada) misclassified as Private and assigned out-of-state Kolkata campus images, alongside multiple cross-state photo leaks.

### Key Corrections:
1. **National Institute Restored**:
   - **Indian Institute of Foreign Trade (IIFT) Kakinada Campus (ID 2802)**: Restored Public/Government (Deemed-to-be-University) classification under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. Purged Kolkata campus photo leak and fake domain crawler string. Set official portal `https://www.iift.ac.in`.
2. **Purged Cross-State Photo Leaks**:
   - **ID 2794 (Akshara IMT Tirupati)**: VJTI Mumbai (Maharashtra) leak purged.
   - **ID 2800 (SALN College Palluru)**: CMC Vellore (Tamil Nadu) hospital campus leak purged.
   - **ID 2806 (Alwar College Visakhapatnam)**: MS Ramaiah Bangalore (Karnataka) leak purged.
3. **Purged Raw Google Search URLs**:
   - **ID 2794 (Akshara IMT Tirupati)**: Replaced raw Google query with official portal `https://www.aksharaimt.com`.
4. **Scraper Hotlink Replacement**:
   - Replaced 5 external scraper webp/hotlinks (collegedunia, studyclap, collegebatch, kollegeapply) with clean local campus architecture assets.
