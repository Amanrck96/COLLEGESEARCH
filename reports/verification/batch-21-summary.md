## 1. Executive Summary & Defect Resolutions

Batch 21 performed an exhaustive forensic audit across three key states/UTs: **Assam** (Dibrugarh, Guwahati), **Chandigarh**, and **Jammu & Kashmir** (Srinagar, Jammu).

1. **Six Major Government Institutions Restored**:
   - **Centre for Management Studies, Dibrugarh University (ID 1667)**: Restored to **`Public/Government (State University Department)`** under Dibrugarh University (Act VIII of 1965).
